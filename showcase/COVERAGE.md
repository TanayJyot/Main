# How many signs would our own dataset need?

Generated by `python showcase/coverage_curve.py`. Do not edit by hand.

Share of everyday signable words (by how often they are used, from `wordfreq`'s English list, which includes film and TV subtitles) that a lexicon has a sign for. Articles, *be*, *do* and *have* are left out because ASL does not sign them as words. One English lemma is counted as one sign, which real ASL does not do exactly, so treat these numbers as estimates.

## The most-used words no dataset we hold can sign
